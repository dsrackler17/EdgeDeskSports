How to read a game

  • View Research opens EdgeDesk’s published article on the game where there is one: its own fair spread, what moved the number, where each team has a measured edge, and what the model could not see. Where the research lives in the Research Terminal, the link says so.
  • Live, Final, Postponed and Canceled come from the schedule feed, with the time it was last read. A game past its start with no update since says Started and shows no score.
  • Research clears EdgeDesk’s research gates: worth opening, not a bet.
  • Watch a real disagreement with the market that has not cleared the gates yet.
  • Pass EdgeDesk and the market agree, or the price leaves no value.
  • Data incomplete something EdgeDesk needs is missing or stale, and the game says what.

Dates are your own calendar day, in your own time zone, and roll over at your midnight. A sport with nothing today says so, and its next games are listed separately under Upcoming, never as today’s. The public read is a free sample of up to eight games at a time. A disagreement is a reason to research a game, never a recommendation to bet it. How every number is built is public: the methodology and the graded public record.
