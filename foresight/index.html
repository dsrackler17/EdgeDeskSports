Market Foresight

Which forecasts, and which observed venues, preceded subsequent market movement? Each identifiable forecast is matched to the DraftKings and Kalshi prices EdgeDesk recorded before and after it was published. You can open any game and see what each source showed, and when.

What this record says, and what it never says

It says “forecast preceded observed movement”, “first observed among tracked venues”, “market moved toward this forecast” or “timing unresolved at available sampling resolution”, and only when the timestamps support it.

It never says that a model caused a move. It never says who led the whole market, because only the tracked venues are observed. Movement toward a forecast does not mean the forecast was right about the result, and a timing advantage is not a profitable or executable trade.

Methodology

The frozen methodology is mf-v1.json (its sha256 is in FROZEN.json). It was committed before any measurement was computed.

Research, not picks. Market Foresight reports when recorded forecasts and recorded prices moved relative to each other. It is not betting advice, it is not a prediction of future results, it does not grade or rank models, and it changes no model, ranking or grade.
