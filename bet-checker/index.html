How a bet is graded

At entry. The moment you log a bet, Bet Checker takes EdgeDesk’s current fair price for exactly that game, market, side and line, and compares the probability it implies with the probability your odds imply.

    If EdgeDesk has no current fair price at exactly your line, the bet is saved without a grade, and the card says why. We never estimate one from a nearby line.

    After the close. Once the game starts, your price is graded against EdgeDesk’s captured no-vig close at your line — the same closing-line value EdgeDesk grades its own signals with. If the market closed at a different line, the card says no close available rather than adjusting anything.
