# Model versus market — validation

Generated by `node tools/market_intel/validation_report.js --write` from committed ledgers; do not edit by hand. Methods and caveats: [`MASTER_PLAN.md`](MASTER_PLAN.md) §3 and the header of [`lib/edgedesk_validation_lab.js`](../../lib/edgedesk_validation_lab.js). Machine-readable: [`validation.json`](validation.json).

**Data through 2026-10-23T20:00:00.000Z.** Every interval is a 95% percentile interval from 2000 cluster-bootstrap draws (seed 20261023); a cluster is a football week or a calendar day (US Eastern) of a slate, because games on one slate share a data refresh and a market regime. No verdict is stated below 30 games in 4 clusters (50 for calibration). Sample states use `lib/edgedesk_validation.js` (a product maturity label, not a scientific verdict).

† = fewer than 10 clusters, so the interval is approximate (a percentile cluster bootstrap under-covers with few clusters). ‡ = the interval excludes zero but leaving one cluster out flips the sign: not robust. A hypothesis is never ACCEPTED on a ‡ result (`judge()` in the lab).

**This is one partial season of live, point-in-time data.** It is the right data (every model number was recorded before kickoff and every market number is a named source at a recorded time), but it is small. An interval that includes zero means *not demonstrated*, not *disproved*.
