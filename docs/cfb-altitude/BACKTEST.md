## Verdict

**Status: REJECTED. Points applied: 0.0.** Statistical support: **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

> Not established: in 13,511 FBS home games 2006–2025, home teams hosting a visitor that climbed 3,000+ ft beat EdgeDesk’s pregame number by +1.03 pts more than other home teams (95% CI −0.18 to +2.23; 651 games at 14 venues). Out of sample the adjustment did not pass its validation gates, so it moves 0 points.

The selected candidate (`cat_3000`, chosen on the 2014–2021 walk-forward only) was then scored on 2022–2025, which nothing was tuned on. On the 139 confirmation games it touched, MAE went from 12.905 to 12.924 (improvement −0.019, 95% CI [−0.079, +0.046], paired p = 0.545). The baseline-preserving fallback therefore stands: V1’s published number is unchanged, and the altitude term runs in SHADOW.

## What was tested, against what

- **Baseline:** football/cfb_p4/engine.js (V1, shipped) replayed cold by football/cfb_p4/research/replay_rows.js, replayed from 2001 (14,213 FBS-vs-FBS games projected, 19 refused for an unrated team). the replay passes no venue geometry, so V1’s home field is its 4.082-pt league constant and travel is 0 — exactly the shipped behaviour for the components that move the number.
- **Residual:** final home margin minus V1’s pregame fair margin. Each candidate is a regression of that residual on pre-registered altitude terms plus an intercept; the intercept absorbs V1’s general home lean and is **never** part of the adjustment, so the altitude term cannot double-count home field.
- **Estimation:** OLS for inference with standard errors clustered by host venue, ridge for the estimate (penalty chosen by forward-chaining inside the training seasons only), then an uncertainty shrink of t²/(1+t²) per coefficient. A term with fewer than 30 games or 5 host venues is held at zero.
- **Market:** sportsdataverse/cfbfastR-data betting/csv/cfb_line_odds.csv.gz via football/cfb_p4/research/build_market.py (cfb_market_orientation_v2). consensus median across books; opening and closing as the archive labels them. No per-quote timestamps exist in the archive, so "closing" is the archive’s final number, not a verified kickoff-minute quote. Licence: a public archive this repository already uses; the underlying sportsbook data’s licence terms are not stated by the archive and were not independently verified.
- **Altitude:** `football/altitude/engine.js` on the schedule’s venue ids and `football/altitude/venues.json`, the same function the live page calls.

**Why an additive altitude term would double count.** Across all non-neutral games V1’s residual is −1.26 pts (95% CI [−1.66, −0.88]): its 4.082-pt league home-field constant over-credits the home side on average. On 3,000+ ft visits the residual is −0.30 ([−1.40, +0.93]): V1 is already about right there. The regression’s "altitude effect" is the gap between the two, so adding it to V1 without also recalibrating V1’s home-field constant would push mountain home teams past where V1 already has them. The football-only margin calibrator (football/cfb_p4/margin_calibration.js, SHADOW) is the place that general lean is measured.

**The closing line at altitude.** On 586 3,000+ ft visits with a close, the home side beat the closing number by +1.40 pts on average (95% CI [+0.31, +2.59], venue-cluster bootstrap over 14 venues), and visitors covered 47.31% of 577 decided games (p = 0.197 against 50%). With only 14 venues the interval is fragile, the cover rate is not distinguishable from a coin flip, and the research package’s fixed-effects ATS estimate was not significant either. It is a monitoring item, not an edge.

The 6x ratio rows are descriptive: most "6x" trips gain under 1,000 ft (sea-level visitors to modest venues), so the ratio mostly flags trips that are not altitude at all.

**Multiple comparisons.** 12 subgroups are shown; at p < 0.05 roughly one would clear by chance alone. 2 did on cover rate ("level (within 1,000 ft)", n 9,299, p 0.013; "gain >= 3,000 ft, ratio < 6x", n 132, p 0.037). Each is post hoc and none was a pre-registered candidate, so none is a finding.

## Gaps

- No per-quote timestamps exist in the line archive: "close" is the archive’s final consensus number, not a verified kickoff-minute quote, and there is no pre-2013 opener for most games.
- Historic stadiums the public provider no longer lists (Hughes, Sam Boyd, Aloha, Qualcomm …) take their elevation from the research package’s CollegeFootballData capture at MEDIUM confidence; the USGS service could not be reached to re-verify them.
- Neutral-site games are described but not modelled: the adjustment was fitted on true home games only.
- Play-level interactions (defensive play load, opponent pace) can only be measured through 2021 and have no live pregame input, so they cannot price even if real.
- The baseline replay carries no quarterback, injury or weather inputs (none exist historically), which is how V1 is replayed everywhere in this repository.
