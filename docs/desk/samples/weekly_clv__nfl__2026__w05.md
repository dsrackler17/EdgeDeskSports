The results did not have to follow, and this week they did not. Against the spread my side went 7-7-1. On totals it went 7-8. That is a losing week, and it stays on the record exactly as it happened.

## The season so far

Season to date, my NFL spread numbers average +0.51 points of CLV across 74 graded games, and 35 of them beat the close (47.3%). Against the spread I am 38-33-5 (53.5%). Break-even at standard juice is 52.4%.

Closing line value is the test I hold my numbers to, because it is graded before the game is played. It is evidence, not proof of an edge, and one season is not enough to call it one.

## How this is graded

- My number is the projection my NFL model published before kickoff (football/nfl/slate.json). Only numbers published before kickoff are recorded.
- The market is nflverse consensus (the slate’s own reference line). The close is nflverse consensus at the final.
- CLV is the distance the market moved toward the side I leaned, from the quote recorded with my number to the close, from the same source. Nothing is estimated: no close, no grade.
- Moneylines carry no CLV grade in my pipeline. Their prices are shown and nothing is computed from them.
- [My live CLV record](https://edgedesksports.com/record.html) and [the football model record, every game (the raw file)](https://edgedesksports.com/record/football/nfl_2026.json) carry every row behind this page.
