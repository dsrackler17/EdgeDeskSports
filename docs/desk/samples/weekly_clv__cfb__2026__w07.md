## How this is graded

- My number is the projection my CFB model published before kickoff (football/fbs/slate.json). Only numbers published before kickoff are recorded.
- The market is ESPN scoreboard line (named book). The close is ESPN line frozen at kickoff; where ESPN keeps none for a finished game, the last ESPN line this record captured before kickoff.
- CLV is the distance the market moved toward the side I leaned, from the quote recorded with my number to the close, from the same source. Nothing is estimated: no close, no grade.
- Moneylines carry no CLV grade in my pipeline. Their prices are shown and nothing is computed from them.
