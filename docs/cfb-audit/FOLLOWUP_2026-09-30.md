## 3. Why EdgeDesk disagrees (item 3)

Each term is an exact piece of the projection, so turning it off moves the number by exactly that much. Terms are credited
undiscounted, and only toward closing the gap.

**North Texas @ Tulsa:** EdgeDesk UNT −9.24, market Tulsa −1.5 (one book). Gap 10.74, 93% explained.

| factor | explains |
|---|---|
| UNT's long-run 2025 rating, still weighted 69% at four games | 7.56 (70%) |
| matchup term: 2025 efficiency carried at half weight | 2.44 (23%) |
| home field above the fitted 2.58; conference | 0 (the home field favours Tulsa) |
| unexplained | 0.74 (7%) |

- Root cause: 2025 North Texas is still counted as 2026, through the prior and the efficiency carry.
- Baylor Hayes (Tulsa's QB) is not read at all. His availability is UNKNOWN, and the college QB term prices 0 pts.
- North Texas's roster inputs are read correctly: returning production at the 1st percentile, new coach.
- #444's discounted explainer leaves 12.87 unexplained.

**Syracuse @ UConn:** EdgeDesk UConn −7.75, market Syracuse −6.5 (stale). Gap 14.25, 29% explained.

| factor | explains |
|---|---|

| home field above the fitted 2.58 (Rentschler gets the 4.08 constant) | 1.50 (11%) |

- Ruled out:
  - misjoined results (all 328 FBS results checked against ESPN);
