# Leave one term out — EdgeDesk college number

Generated by `football/cfb_p4/research/loto.js` from `football/validation/loto_cfb.json` (2026-10-26). Do not edit by hand: rerun the harness. Declared 2026-10-24, before the first run.

**The question.** For every term that moves the college number: does the shipped engine predict games it never saw better WITH the term than without it, and is the term’s weight right? Nothing is refitted: each term is removed from the shipped projection, game by game, and the repository’s own promotion rules (`football/validation/promote.js`) judge the difference. The weight is the multiplier k that best fits the result with everything else held at its shipped value: k ≈ 1 is right, k ≈ 0 carries nothing, k < 0 points the wrong way.

**The rows.** 8,724 completed FBS-vs-FBS games, 2014–2025, priced by `disagreement_replay.js`: the shipped engine replayed cold and frozen weekly, with the matchup layer fed by the board’s own efficiency adapter. Held out: 2022–2025. Leakage check: clean on all 10,009 rows.

- **Team strength:** the base of the model: dropping it is not a question, only its weight is.

The live board starts from the 2025 closing seed and resets those features to league average at the break into 2026, so it prices the same term. ## Reading it

- error = final home margin minus the projection; + delta = the term makes held-out predictions better. The close is the archive’s final consensus line (cfb_market_orientation_v2), a lower-noise second target.
- Brier with one normal per game at the row’s own sigma for both arms, because the replay stores the engine’s win probability for the full projection only.
- Dropping a term without refitting the rest answers "is the shipped term worth its points". A term that fails here could still be worth something with new weights; that is a refit, judged by the same rules on a later window.
