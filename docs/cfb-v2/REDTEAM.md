# CFB V2 red team: final report

This report is generated by `python3 -m v2.rt_report` from `football/cfb_v2/research/report/redteam/*.json`.

**Premise:** V2 was assumed wrong until the evidence said otherwise.

**How it was attacked:** in six ways.
- **Frozen and rebuilt:** candidate 001 was frozen and rebuilt from a fresh download.
- **Re-implemented:** it was re-implemented independently.
- **Poisoned:** its inputs were poisoned with future data, to see what moves.
- **Injected:** its models were offered hindsight columns under realistic names.
- **Every model and feature challenged:** every component, stacker and feature group was challenged.
- **Market-tested:** its probabilities and market claims were tested against the opener, the close and a coin-flip reality check.

**What changed:** only three kinds of change were made.
- fixes for things that were wrong;
- removals that pre-registered, development-only rules demanded;
- a data-measured recalibration of the QB overlay.

**Adding nothing new:** no feature was added, as the brief required.

**Three more problems found by audit:**
- **Market QA looked at the close.** It dropped openers that were sign-flipped or more than 14 points from the close, i.e. it used the close to decide which openers existed. Fixed: those flags are now evaluation-only, and the engine's point-in-time orientation guard decides instead.

## 2. Sign-convention audit

**Canonical convention:** home margin > 0 means the home team is expected to win by that many points. A book line of Texas Tech −7 at home becomes internal +7; the same line with Texas Tech on the road becomes −7. The conversion happens once, at ingestion.

**What `tests_signs.py` (12 of 12 checks pass) and the engine `tests.js` sign block cover:**
- 9 hand-graded scenarios, run through every consumer: home favourite; road favourite; home favourite, model takes the dog; road dog loses by more than the line; favourite flip; pick'em push; pick'em win; hook loses by half a point; whole-number push;
- both converters: the archive, which is already margin convention and must not be negated again, and the CFBD feed, which is book convention;
- cover grading, EV, CLV and the break-even price;
- alternate spreads (now excluded from the consensus);
- the market-moved-toward-model label;
- mirror symmetry.

**On the real archive** (the checks assert each of these):
- Home and road favourites win in every source and every site type (cfbfa/H n=12743, cfbfa/N n=505, cfbd_/H n=315).
- Pick'em games split close to 50/50, and after a favourite flip the closing favourite wins more often (pickem n=341, flips n=360).
- Graded rows match a longhand implementation exactly (n=6062).

**Result:** no sign bug was found.

## 3. Walk-forward methodology

**Seasons:**
- **2009-2011:** burn-in (variance components, QB shrinkage).
- **2012 onward:** the first frozen feature rows.
- **2014 onward:** out-of-fold components.
- **2016 onward:** stacking.
- **2017 onward:** calibration.
- **Development:** 2016-2023, where every choice is made.
- **Holdout:** 2024-2025.
- **Live:** 2026.

**The rules:**
- For season S, every fit uses seasons < S only: expanding window, never a random split.
- Features are frozen at each week's Tuesday 12:00 UTC freeze, from games that kicked off before it.
- There is one prediction per game and freeze, written once.

**Independent reproduction.** `rt_walkforward.py` re-implements the loop without the original code: scikit-learn ridge, LightGBM called directly, and its own stackers, error model and calibrators. Against candidate 001's frozen record (11262 games, stored to 4 decimals, so 5e-5 is the resolution) the largest absolute differences are:
- components: A_adj_eff 5.0e-05, B_elo 5.0e-05, C_ridge 5.0e-05, D_gbm 5.0e-05, E_drive 5.0e-05;
- the ensemble 8.7e-05, sigma 5.0e-05, reliability 7.3e-05;
- interval bounds 8.0e-05; raw win probability 5.0e-05.

**A contamination this check caught.** Hardening added a production drop list (`config.DROPPED_FEATURES`) inside `models.features_for`, which silently removed the five dropped features from the red team's "candidate 001" spec too. This check then showed ridge and GBM differences of up to 4 points. The drop list is now applied only on production paths; the red-team spec is explicit (`drop=()` for candidate 001), a test pins that, and every candidate-001 phase computed after the change was re-run.

**Reproduction from scratch.** Before freezing, the whole pipeline was rebuilt from a fresh data download. Every development and holdout metric matched the committed backtest to within optimizer tolerance.

**The holdout has been used.** The 2024-2025 holdout was scored for candidate 001 before this red team, and its diagnostics were visible during the audit. Every hardening choice therefore followed pre-registered, development-only rules ([`HARDENING_PREREG.md`](HARDENING_PREREG.md)). The only untouched evidence is 2026.

### Week-to-week learning (candidate 001)

Each team is rated at every freeze by the ensemble's neutral-site margin against a league-average reference team, built with the production snapshot code.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| A sustainable: beat expectation by 14+, success margin >= +4, turnover margin <= +1 | 1174 | 2.74 | 1.94 | 1.49 [0.59, 2.43] |
| B luck: beat expectation by 14+, turnover margin >= +3 or ST >= +7, success margin <= 0 | 203 | 1.88 | 3.02 | -1.15 [-3.39, 1.06] |
| C lost, but won the success battle (success margin >= +3) | 897 | -0.43 | -1.83 | 0.49 [-0.69, 1.57] |
| D lost and lost the success battle (success margin <= -3) | 4315 | -1.72 | -1.35 | -0.68 [-1.18, -0.18] |
| E won, but lost the success battle (success margin <= -3) | 897 | 0.41 | 1.81 | 0.13 [-0.89, 1.25] |

**At equal surprise:** a turnover- or special-teams-driven result moves the rating 0.70 points less than a sustainable one [0.55, 0.85]. The scoreboard Elo does the opposite even before adjusting for surprise: it moves 3.02 for the luck class and 1.94 for the sustainable one.

**Automated tests:**
- a loss that won the success battle is not a downgrade on average (C >= -0.25): **FAIL**
- a loss that won the success battle is not punished like one that lost it (C > D): **PASS**
- no class is systematically mis-rated in its next game (every next-game residual CI covers 0): **FAIL**
- sustainable surprise moves the rating more than luck (A > B at equal surprise): **PASS**

**Reading:** the model learns the right *direction*.
- It rewards sustainable efficiency more than luck.
- It barely downgrades a loss that won the success battle; the next game says that small downgrade was fine.
- **It under-reacts to sustained efficiency.** Teams that won big on efficiency beat V2 by 1.49 points the following week, and teams that lost and lost the success battle fall 0.68 short of it.
- This is the same constant-prior-weight problem found in the early-season analysis.

## 21. Shadow-mode implementation

V2 runs **beside** V1 and changes nothing V1 does. Every weekly freeze (Tuesday 12:00 UTC) writes a write-once, hashed row per upcoming game. Each row carries:
- the hardened V2 projection, its distribution and components;
- candidate 001's projection, from its own hash-locked artifacts;
- V1's projection, as published on the FBS board;
- the market as observed by the run that froze it;
- the pregame availability, QB-availability and named-starter evidence the V1 board held at the freeze (recorded only; V2 does not read it).

**Afterwards, separately:**
- An **append-only line ledger** timestamps every observed line change.
- A **derived outcomes file** records the closing number, the final result, every model's error, CLV and grading.
- The **production engine** computes the research status (BET/LEAN/REVIEW/PASS).

**Monitoring** (`monitoring.json`, rendered by `football/cfb_v2/monitor.html`) covers, per week:
- MAE, RMSE, Brier and calibration;
- V1 vs V2 vs candidate 001 on the same games;
- model vs the line at the freeze, and vs the close;
- CLV and ATS;
- statuses and pass rate;
- largest misses;
- model disagreement.

**Health warnings:**
- MAE spike and calibration;
- missing play-by-play;
- stale odds and a stale availability source;
- BET while disabled;
- ensemble weights;
- C/D disagreement;
- stale pipeline;
- week-matched output drift, tested against a sampling-noise null.

A warning informs a person; it never retrains anything. The frozen predictions are never modified.

## 22. Remaining weaknesses

1. **Less accurate than the market** in every window: V2 hardened trails the opener by 0.23 points on development, 0.27 on the holdout and 0.76 on the 208 games of 2026 to date.
2. **The prior fade has the wrong shape.** Priors are under-used early and over-used mid-season, and the model under-reacts to sustained efficiency.
3. **The P4 side of P4-vs-G5 games is underrated:** -2.6 points on development and -3.3 on the holdout. So are market favourites of more than 21 points (-1.7 and -2.6).
4. **No cover skill.** Calibrated cover probabilities are indistinguishable from a coin flip.
5. **No pregame QB-status, injury or weather history before 2026.** The live overlays are measured (QB) or declared (injury, weather), never validated on pregame reports. The frozen shadow rows now keep the pregame availability and QB evidence, so validation can start after 2026.
6. **FBS-vs-FCS games are not priced.** The mean is biased against the FBS side (-6.1 points on the holdout), and the intervals are honest only because they are about 47 points wide.
7. **The holdout is no longer pristine.** 2024-2025 has been scored for two versions; only 2026 onward is untouched.
8. **No historical spread prices after 2019.** ROI for 2020-2025 assumes −110. The 2014-2019 closing prices are real.
9. **The provider's EPA model** is fitted on historical seasons: fixed, unremovable value-function hindsight.
