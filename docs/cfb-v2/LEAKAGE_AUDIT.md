# CFB V2 leakage audit

This file is generated by `python3 -m v2.leakage_audit`.

**How it was checked:** every feature that any submodel of candidate 001 reads was audited. It was then **attacked** in two ways:
- **Future poisoning:** season 2019 was rebuilt with every later game and season randomised (plays, EPA, scores, QB starters, later rosters, talent and coaching). Every pre-cutoff feature had to come out bit-identical.
- **Injection:** each kind of hindsight was offered to the models under realistic names.

**Result: four problems found and fixed.**
- a whole-season median fill;
- batch-median fills at prediction time;
- a market QA step that used the closing line;
- a name-prefix layer contract.

None of them touched the point prediction. The volatility and fill leaks affect intervals and probabilities only. The market QA leak affected only which openers were evaluated. The contract hole was never exploited: no such column existed.

### ratings

- **Verdict:** CLEAN (proved end-to-end by the poisoning test)
- **Q1. Source** sportsdataverse espn_cfb_pbp play-by-play -> stage-1 team-game efficiency -> stage-3 joint opponent-adjusted Bayesian ridge ratings
- **Q2. Available at** plays of games that kicked off BEFORE the Tuesday 12:00 UTC freeze of the target game's week (asserted in build_ratings: obs.kickoff_ts < T; artifact test counts only prior games)
- **Q3. Contains the target game?** No. The target game kicks off after its freeze, so none of its plays exist at T. tests_poison (all 841 features bit-identical before the cutoff when every later game/season is randomised)
- **Q4. Season aggregate recomputed with future games?** No. Ratings are re-solved at every freeze from games before it; never a season total. tests_poison (all 841 features bit-identical before the cutoff when every later game/season is randomised)
- **Q5. Injury/depth hindsight?** Not applicable (no injury or depth data enters).
- **Q6. Market later than the prediction?** Not applicable. The provider's win-probability and spread columns are excluded at read time (FORBIDDEN_PBP_COLUMNS; test pbp_never_reads_market_or_wp_columns).
- **Q7. Ranking hindsight?** Not applicable (no rankings).
- **Q8. Future opponent performance?** No. Opponent ratings are solved jointly at the same freeze from the same past games. tests_poison (all 841 features bit-identical before the cutoff when every later game/season is randomised)
- **Q9. Preprocessing fit on the full dataset?** Variance components from the 2009-2011 burn-in only; metric scales (standardisation of edges) from data-only finals of the three seasons BEFORE the target season.
- **Q10. Normalisation uses future seasons?** No: metric_scales(S) reads seasons S-3..S-1 only.
- **Caveat:** EPA values come from the provider's fixed expected-points model, fitted by the provider on historical seasons that overlap the development window. It is a value function of down, distance and field position (not of any game's outcome), but it is hindsight about how football is valued. Cannot be removed without a re-fitted EP model; disclosed.
