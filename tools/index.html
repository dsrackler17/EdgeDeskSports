Why these tools

EdgeDesk is a research terminal, not a picks service. Its whole method is to price a game before looking at a sportsbook and then measure the disagreement honestly. These three tools are the free, public edge of that method: the same de-vig arithmetic the terminal uses as its market benchmark, the same break-even arithmetic its EV uses, and the same research statuses its board shows.

Each calculator prints its working, so you can check it — and none of them claims to know who will win.
