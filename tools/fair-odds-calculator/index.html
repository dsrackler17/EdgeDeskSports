Your probability

A push is a refunded bet — a spread landing exactly on a whole number. Leave it blank for a moneyline or a half-point line.

How a fair price is worked out

A price is fair when, over many bets at that probability, it would neither win nor lose money. That happens when the decimal odds equal one divided by the probability: 50% is 2.00 (+100), 60% is 1.667 (−150), 40% is 2.50 (+150).

Questions

Where does the probability come from?

From you. This tool does arithmetic on the number you type; it does not know anything about the game. Positive expected value here means only “if your probability is right, this price pays more than it should” — which is the whole question.

Why does −110 need 52.38%?

At −110 you risk $110 to win $100 (decimal 1.909). To break even you must win 1 ÷ 1.909 = 52.38% of the time. The no-vig calculator shows how much of that is the sportsbook’s margin.
