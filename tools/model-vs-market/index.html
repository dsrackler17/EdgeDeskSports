How to read a row

FieldWhat it is
EdgeDesk fairThe spread EdgeDesk’s model makes the game from football information alone. The market never enters it.
MarketA captured sportsbook line, with the book and when it was taken. A price older than its window is called stale, not current.
GapEdgeDesk’s number minus the market’s, in points, toward the team EdgeDesk likes more than the market does.
ReliabilityHow complete, fresh and consistent the inputs are (0–100). It is not a probability and not a chance of winning.

The four statuses

  • Research — clears EdgeDesk’s research gates: worth opening, not a bet.
  • Watch — a real disagreement that has not cleared the gates yet (for example a large gap that failed an integrity check).
  • Pass — EdgeDesk and the market agree, or the price leaves no value.
  • Data incomplete — something EdgeDesk needs is missing or stale, and the row says what.

A disagreement is a reason to research a game, never a recommendation to bet it. Large gaps are more often caused by something the model cannot see — an injury, a quarterback change, weather — than by an edge, which is why the biggest ones are held at Watch until they pass EdgeDesk’s integrity checks.
