    How Fairline measures

    Fair price. EdgeDesk’s de-vigged price for one exact game, market, side and line — the same fair Bet Checker grades with. A price at another line is a different bet, and is never compared with it.

    Edge vs fair. The fair probability minus the probability the price implies, in points. Positive means the price is better than fair.

    Closing-line value. Your decimal odds times EdgeDesk’s no-vig closing probability at your line, minus one — the same CLV EdgeDesk grades its own signals with. A bet whose close was not captured has no CLV, and a live bet is never graded against a pregame close.

    The Tape’s week. Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00, Central time. A bet belongs to the week its game started (a bet with no known start, the week it was placed). “What changed” compares your slices over the bets before the week with your slices through it.

    Small samples. A slice of your bets gets a label only once it has enough settled bets, and its label speaks about the close only once enough of them have a captured close. Until then it says so.
