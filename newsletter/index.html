What is in it

The number, and the book’s number

Two or three sentences built from the model’s own published matchup reads and measured advantages. No invented injuries, no recalled history, no filler.

What this is not

It is not a picks service and it does not sell one. There are no locks, no confidence percentages the model does not publish, and no claim that a disagreement with a book is an edge — EdgeDesk’s own NFL model does not beat the closing line out of sample, and every edition says so. What the email gives you is a shortlist and the evidence, so the research you do next starts somewhere useful.
