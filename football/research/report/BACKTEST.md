# EdgeDesk Football Engine — walk-forward backtest report

Generated by `gen_report.py` from `report_nfl.json` / `report_cfb.json` (themselves produced by `train_nfl.py` / `train_cfb.py`). Chronological walk-forward only; hyperparameters frozen on seasons ≤2015; 2016–2025 untouched. **No random splits anywhere.**

## The headline, stated plainly

**The NFL model does NOT beat the closing line out of sample.** Against the closing consensus spread it goes 647-672 (49.05%) at 2+ points of disagreement (p=0.7544); totals reach 51.7% at 3+ points (p=0.156) — suggestive, not significant. Only historical CLOSING lines are publicly archived (no openers), so no CLV backtest is possible; beating the close is a strictly harder test than beating an opener, and the engine ships labeled UNPROVEN until its own graded CLV against real quotes says otherwise. No CFB line archive exists at all, so CFB claims exactly nothing about the market.
