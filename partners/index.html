Research partnerships

If you write about college football or the NFL, EdgeDesk can give your readers something most football content does not have: a model’s own number for the game, beside the market’s, with the reasons behind it and the things the model could not see printed next to both.

What EdgeDesk publishes that you can work with

A number before the market

A fair spread for every FBS and NFL game — and a projected score where the model publishes a total — built from football information alone. The sportsbook line never enters it.

The reasons, measured

Which inputs moved the number (team strength, home field, matchup, rest, quarterback) and by how much — never an invented injury or a recalled “trend”.

The limits, printed

Data confidence, reliability, what was missing from the build, and an itemised list of what the model could not measure.

A record that is graded

Pregame numbers are frozen and graded against the close and the result in the public record — including what EdgeDesk got wrong.

Ways to work together

  • Cite the free research. Every research article and the model-vs-market explorer is public, with no account. Link to it freely.
  • Evaluate the terminal. Tell us about your publication and we can talk about access for evaluating the full research terminal.
  • A partnership. Content, data or referral arrangements are agreed individually and in writing, before anything goes live. EdgeDesk does not pay or credit anything without that written agreement.

What EdgeDesk will not do: supply picks or bet recommendations, put a promise about outcomes in front of your readers, or market to anyone under 21.
