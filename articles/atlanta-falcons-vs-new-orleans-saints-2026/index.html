What EdgeDesk could not measure

  • Offensive-line, receiver, linebacker and secondary grades — EdgeDesk’s position-group player layer is college-only. There is no NFL unit grade in this repo, so the trench and coverage sections report the trench and coverage facts the NFL model does carry — sacks taken, sacks generated, EPA allowed by phase, explosive rates — and claim nothing beyond them.
  • Special teams — the NFL rating state carries no special-teams feature at all. It is absent from the projection and from every comparison above.
  • Coaching, scheme and play-calling tendency — no public feed for it is wired into this model; a new coordinator changes a team in ways these ratings only learn after the fact.
  • Snap counts and personnel packages — not present in the public team-week feed this model reads.
  • Individual player grades — the NFL layer rates TEAMS and quarterbacks. No other individual player carries a rating here.
  • Who is actually available — EdgeDesk has not loaded an NFL injury report in this session.

Unknown is never scored as zero-and-forgotten. Where the model has no input it contributes no points, and the gap is carried here instead of being filled in.
