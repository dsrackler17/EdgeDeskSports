Why Boston College can keep it close

  • Playing at Boston College is worth 4.1 points — the league-wide home-field number, because a per-venue table was tested and could not be told apart from the home team’s own quality (+4.1 pts).
  • Boston College is the better football team by 0.8 points on opponent-adjusted results — but about 86% of that rating is still last season carried forward, and in a portal era last season is a different team (+0.8 pts).

  • It is early enough that both ratings still lean on preseason belief — it widens the outcome range by about 6%.
