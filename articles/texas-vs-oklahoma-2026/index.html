  • When Oklahoma has the ball (pass protection against the pass rush): Oklahoma’s offense has allowed a sack on 6.7% of its dropbacks, and Texas’ defense sacks the quarterback on 11.0% of opponent dropbacks (FBS average 5.6%).
  • When Oklahoma has the ball (the run game against the run defense): Oklahoma’s offense averages 3.5 yards per carry, and Texas’ defense allows 4.1 yards per carry (FBS average 5.1).

What points toward Oklahoma
