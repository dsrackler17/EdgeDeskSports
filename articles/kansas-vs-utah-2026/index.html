What points toward Utah

  • When Utah has the ball (pass protection against the pass rush): Utah’s offense has allowed a sack on 3.1% of its dropbacks, and Kansas’ defense sacks the quarterback on 5.9% of opponent dropbacks (FBS average 5.6%).
  • When Kansas has the ball (the passing game against the pass defense): Kansas’ offense averages 7.4 yards per pass attempt, and Utah’s defense allows 4.6 yards per pass attempt (FBS average 7.5).
  • When Kansas has the ball (big plays on the ground): Kansas’ offense turns 5.2% of its runs into explosive gains, and Utah’s defense gives up explosive gains on 3.7% of opponent runs (FBS average 6.6%).

What points toward Kansas

  • When Utah has the ball (big plays on the ground): Utah’s offense turns 7.6% of its runs into explosive gains, and Kansas’ defense gives up explosive gains on 3.6% of opponent runs (FBS average 6.6%).
