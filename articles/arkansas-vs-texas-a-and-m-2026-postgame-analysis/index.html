How this makes the next read better

THE NOISE: a 27-point margin. Whatever the scoreboard says, none of that is a repeatable property of either team, and weighting it in the next look at these sides would be learning the wrong thing.

THE ASSUMPTION THAT CANNOT BE CHECKED: 9 of the claims in the original article rest on signals no box score in this repository publishes. Those parts of the number are carried on faith between one model rebuild and the next, and it is worth knowing which parts those are.

THE TOTAL: EdgeDesk missed the scoring by 14 points. A margin can be right while a total is badly wrong, and they come from different halves of the model — so a total miss is a question about pace and finishing, not about which team is better.
