The case for each team

These are football cases assembled from measured states. Neither is a bet, and neither is advice.

Why Missouri can pull away

  • Missouri is the better football team by 6.0 points on opponent-adjusted results — but about 86% of that rating is still last season carried forward, and in a portal era last season is a different team (-6.0 pts).
  • Conference strength differs and the in-season sample is still thin, worth 2.8 points to Missouri (-2.8 pts).

Why Kansas can keep it close

  • Playing at Kansas is worth 4.1 points — the league-wide home-field number, because a per-venue table was tested and could not be told apart from the home team’s own quality (+4.1 pts).
  • The outcome range is 15.5 points wide at one sigma — p10 -28.6 to p90 +16.4 for Kansas, so a projection of -4.6 still produces results a long way either side of it.
  • It is early enough that both ratings still lean on preseason belief — it widens the outcome range by about 6%.
  • 60% of the model’s own input contract was empty for this game — no starter, injury or per-player talent context reached it, so the number leans on team strength more than it should.
