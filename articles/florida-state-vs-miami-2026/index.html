What points toward Miami

  • When Miami has the ball (big plays through the air): Miami’s offense turns 15.8% of its pass plays into explosive gains, and Florida State’s defense gives up explosive gains on 9.6% of opponent pass plays (FBS average 10.4%).

  • When Florida State has the ball (the run game against the run defense): Florida State’s offense averages 5.3 yards per carry, and Miami’s defense allows 2.8 yards per carry (FBS average 5.1).

What points toward Florida State

  • When Miami has the ball (pass protection against the pass rush): Miami’s offense has allowed a sack on 3.2% of its dropbacks, and Florida State’s defense sacks the quarterback on 9.3% of opponent dropbacks (FBS average 5.6%).
