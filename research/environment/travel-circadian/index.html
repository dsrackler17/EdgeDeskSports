The early-kickoff body clock: the line caught up

NFL West Coast teams in the early Eastern window score about 4.5 points below their ratings, with a wide interval. Through 2011 the spread missed it and they covered 41.3% of the time. Since 2012 the line has priced it and the edge is gone.

Priced by the market since 2012NFL 2000–2025 · CFB 2001–2021Updated 2026-10-17
Research questionDoes a West Coast team traveling east for a 1:00 p.m. ET kickoff perform differently from a team traveling the same distance west?

Findings

  • NFL visitors who travel two or three zones east for an early body-clock kickoff land 4.5 points below their ratings (-4.5 [-9.3, +0.4], p = 0.11, 623 games). The interval is wide but the estimate is stable: -4.7 before 2012 and -4.6 after.
  • Against the spread, those teams finished 3.2 points worse than the line from 2000 to 2011 (p = 0.001) and covered 41.3% of 283. From 2012 to 2025 the gap is 0.0 points (p = 0.97) and the cover rate is 52.8% of 322.
  • Matched for distance, the direction of travel does not show a clean asymmetry against the line in either league. NFL 2–3 zone east trips in the afternoon: -0.8 [-3.6, +1.9]; west trips by day: +0.4 [-1.1, +1.9].
  • Night games point the classic way in the NFL (westerners playing earlier on their body clock do better, gap +3.4 [-0.4, +7.2] vs the spread, p = 0.10) but the opposite way in college (-1.8 [-4.8, +1.2]), so it is not a stable signal.
  • College shows no early-kickoff effect against the spread (+1.0 [-1.9, +3.9]) or ratings (-0.5 [-4.5, +3.5]), on only 151 games.
  • There is no hangover in the NFL game after a long trip: +0.2 [-0.2, +0.7] points vs the spread.

What this means for the EdgeDesk model

Model statusNot a betting edge. The early-kickoff effect is real on the field but has been priced by NFL closing lines since 2012. It enters the combined environment model as a candidate input to the fair margin (signed body-clock kickoff and eastbound time-zone shift), where it must beat the closing line out of sample before it can move any EdgeDesk number. The night-game asymmetry is not stable across leagues and is not carried forward.

Data and what was not available

NFL games
6,924 regular and postseason games at a home stadium, 2000–2025 (1999 has no kickoff times). Neutral and international games excluded.
CFB games
13,013 non-neutral FBS-vs-FBS games 2001–2021 at their verified venue; 8,115 with spreads (2006–2019).
Time zones
Venue and visitor home-stadium IANA time zones, converted on the game date so daylight-saving changes and Arizona are handled.
Kickoff
NFL: nflverse kickoff time (Eastern). CFB: CollegeFootballData start time (UTC).
Lines
NFL: nflverse spread_line. CFB: SBR archive, Pinnacle where available. Neither is a licensed closing feed with a documented capture time.
Strength
Team-season rating for each side estimated jointly with the travel effects; SEs clustered by home team (NFL) or host venue (CFB).
Actual travel

Departure day, flight time and hotel arrival are not public. Distance and time zones stand in for them.

Sleep and practice schedules

Teams that fly a day early or keep home-clock schedules cannot be identified.

CFB kickoff placeholders

About 9% of CFB kickoffs sit at exactly 00:00 UTC. Excluding them changes the main estimate little (-1.0 [-5.2, +3.2] vs -0.5 [-4.5, +3.5]).

International games

London, Germany and Mexico City games are excluded; three of those stadiums lack verified coordinates.
