The bye edge is real, and the market caught up

Across 6,539 NFL regular-season games and 12,167 FBS games, we measured byes, short weeks, Thursday mini-byes, road trips and overtime hangovers against team strength and against the closing spread.

Priced by the marketNFL 1999–2025 · CFB 2001–2021Updated 2026-10-17
Research questionIs the advantage of extra rest real, or has the betting market already priced it in?

Findings

  • An NFL team off a bye beats its strength-adjusted expectation by +1.29 points against a normally rested opponent (95% CI [+0.38, +2.20], p = 0.01). The spread moves by +0.74 points for the same situation.
  • Against the market the bye edge is +0.47 [-0.35, +1.29] points ATS (p = 0.26). Teams off a bye covered 51.6% of 630 graded games (47.6% to 55.4%).
  • By era, the NFL bye was worth +2.14 [+0.93, +3.34] points in 1999–2010 and beat the spread by +1.54 [+0.29, +2.79] (p = 0.02). In 2011–2025 it is +0.54 [-0.71, +1.79] on the scoreboard and -0.47 [-1.61, +0.67] against the spread.
  • The bye effect on the scoreboard changed by -1.49 [-3.12, +0.15] points from 1999–2010 to 2011–2025 (p = 0.07). The decline points the way the shrinking-bye claim says, but it is not statistically clear on its own.
  • Short weeks barely exist as a mismatch: Thursday games are short week against short week, so only 9 NFL games put a short-rested team against a rested one and the estimate (+0.64 [-8.05, +9.33]) is uninformative. Six-day teams (off Monday night) are -0.57 [-1.70, +0.56] points.
  • Road trips and overtime: a 3rd straight road game is +0.48 [-1.10, +2.07] points for the NFL visitor; coming off overtime is -0.36 [-1.57, +0.85]. Both are small samples.
  • College: a bye is worth +1.22 [+0.56, +1.89] points against expectation and +0.29 [-0.53, +1.11] points against the spread (p = 0.51). Teams off a bye covered 50.7% of 1,312 games.
  • Each extra day of rest differential is worth +0.20 [+0.09, +0.31] NFL points on the scoreboard and +0.07 [-0.02, +0.17] against the spread.

What this means for the EdgeDesk model

Model statusThe early-era ATS edge on byes is one test among dozens here and has not held since 2011, so it is treated as history, not a signal. Rest differential stays in the EdgeDesk research card as observed context. It does not get its own adjustment to the fair spread, because the market spread already moves by about the measured amount and the ATS residual is not distinguishable from zero. The combined environment model tests it jointly with travel, body clock and weather before any adjustment is allowed.
