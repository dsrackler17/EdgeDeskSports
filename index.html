FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is EdgeDesk a picks service?

No. EdgeDesk is a sports market research and decision-support platform. It shows its own price, the market’s price, why they differ and what could make it wrong — and inside the terminal its decision engine classifies the current price as BET, LEAN, WATCH or PASS, with the exact line and book when a price qualifies and the price that would change the answer when it does not. Every decision says why, and the choice to bet stays yours — no picks, no locks, no guaranteed winners.

What does EdgeDesk cover?

Football: the NFL and NCAA FBS, the top division of college football, in one terminal — game markets (spreads, moneylines, totals) and player props.

Which player props does EdgeDesk analyze?

Passing yards, passing touchdowns, interceptions, rushing yards, rushing attempts, receiving yards, receptions, touchdowns and other supported NFL and FBS markets, whenever sportsbooks post them. Each prop is read beside its game. Prop markets are in EdgeDesk’s experimental validation stage: their EV is labelled model-estimated and they carry no stake until their graded record promotes them.

How does the free trial work?

You get the full product for 7 days — no locked tiers, no trial-only board. Stripe holds a card and charges nothing until day 8. We email you before the trial converts, with the exact date you will be charged, and if you cancel during the trial you are never charged.

Where does EdgeDesk get its numbers?

It builds its own. A football model built from opponent-adjusted efficiency per play, home field and a learned quarterback layer; a player-prop model built from volume, share and efficiency, adjusted for matchup, game script, weather and availability; and sportsbook prices captured with the time they were captured. A language model never produces a probability, a price or a rating.

What is EdgeDesk EV?

It compares EdgeDesk’s estimated probability with the sportsbook’s actual price and break-even probability, to estimate the value available at that exact price. Expected value estimates the theoretical return of repeatedly taking the same price if EdgeDesk’s probability is accurate — it does not guarantee profit. Raw and calibrated EV are shown separately. More in the methodology.

Does EdgeDesk guarantee winning bets?

No, and nobody honestly can. EdgeDesk EV is an estimate built on EdgeDesk’s probability — if the probability is wrong, so is the EV. That is why the uncertainty, the drivers and the graded public record sit right beside it.

What happens when the model is uncertain?

It says so on the game. An unconfirmed starting quarterback is carried as unknown, never as healthy. A stale price is not compared. A very large gap is investigated as a possible data problem rather than surfaced as a find. Layers that have not cleared validation move no line.

What does “founding member” mean?

Founding members keep their rate while continuously subscribed. There is one plan and everyone gets everything; nothing about it is limited-time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Settings › Subscription inside the app, one click, no conversation. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged; cancel later and access runs to the end of the period you already paid for.

What is the public record?

EdgeDesk’s research, captured before kickoff, frozen and graded automatically afterward. It is public — no account, no paywall — and the losing stretches stay in it.
