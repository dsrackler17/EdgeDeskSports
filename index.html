MAX EV is not a recommendation: aggressive lines lean hardest on the tails of the distribution. EdgeDesk EV prices every captured quote at its own line and price — and any line and price you type in.

What EV means. Expected value estimates the theoretical return of repeatedly taking the same price if EdgeDesk’s probability is accurate. It is an estimate — it does not guarantee profit.

It shows its work first. EV is only as good as the probability under it, so the drivers, the uncertainty and the public grading sit beside it, and raw and calibrated EV are shown separately.

The rules are written down. A price qualifies at 1+ point of probability edge, positive EV and no worse than −125 — EdgeDesk’s pre-registered EV policy. It runs in shadow: every read is recorded and graded, and none is presented as a bet.

Most prices don’t qualify. On a real slate, PASS is the most common answer. That is a normal result, not a broken one.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is EdgeDesk a picks service?

No. EdgeDesk is a sports market research and decision-support platform. It shows its own price, the market’s price, why they differ and what could make it wrong — and its decision engine classifies the current price as BET, LEAN, WATCH or PASS, with the exact line, book and a conservative unit size when a price qualifies, and the price that would change the answer when it does not. Every decision says why, and the choice to bet stays yours — no picks, no locks, no guaranteed winners.

What sports does EdgeDesk cover?

Football: the NFL and NCAA FBS, the top division of college football, in one terminal.

How does the 7-day trial work?

You get the full product for 7 days — no locked tiers, no trial-only board. Stripe holds a card and charges nothing until day 8. We email you before the trial converts, and if you cancel in the 7 days you are never charged.

Where does EdgeDesk get its numbers?

It builds its own. A football model built from opponent-adjusted efficiency per play, home field and a learned quarterback layer; player and roster ratings built from public play-by-play and participation data; and sportsbook prices captured with the time they were captured. A language model never produces a probability, a price or a rating.

What is EdgeDesk EV?

It compares EdgeDesk’s estimated probability with the sportsbook’s actual price and break-even probability, to estimate the value available at that exact price. Raw and calibrated EV are shown separately, and only the calibrated number can clear EdgeDesk’s price rules.

Why isn’t the model spread enough?

Because Auburn −8 at −105 and Auburn −8 at −130 are economically different bets. A model edge only becomes a betting edge at a price. EdgeDesk evaluates the price, not merely the side.

Does EdgeDesk guarantee winning bets?

No, and nobody honestly can. EdgeDesk EV is an estimate built on EdgeDesk’s probability — if the probability is wrong, so is the EV. That is why the uncertainty, the drivers and the graded public record sit right beside it.

What happens when the model is uncertain?

It says so on the game. An unconfirmed starting quarterback is carried as unknown, never as healthy. A stale price is not compared. A very large gap is investigated as a possible data problem rather than surfaced as a find. Layers that have not cleared validation move no line.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Settings › Subscription inside the app, one click, no conversation. Cancel during the 7 days and you are never charged; cancel later and access runs to the end of the period you already paid for.

What is the public record?

EdgeDesk’s research, captured before kickoff, frozen and graded automatically afterward. It is public — no account, no paywall — and the losing stretches stay in it.
