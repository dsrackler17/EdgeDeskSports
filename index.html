Division of labor

AI that interprets the evidence. Not AI that invents the numbers.

The quantitative work is deterministic and auditable. The model never produces a probability, a fair price, an edge, or a CLV figure — it reads the packet the engine built and explains what's in it.

Engine Owns every number
  • De-vigging and fair probability across the book
  • Fair price and edge versus what's offered
  • Line movement, steam and stale-price detection
  • Closing line value, graded automatically after the close
Model Owns none of them
  • Reads the packet and explains what it says
  • Points at which evidence is load-bearing
  • Names what would change the conclusion
  • Refuses questions the evidence can't answer

Closing line value

Winning tonight doesn't prove your process works. Did you beat the close?

The closing line is the market's most accurate price. If you consistently bought a better number than the one the market settled on, your process is finding something. If you didn't, a winning night was luck wearing a disguise. It's the only edge signal that exists before results do.
