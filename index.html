The research terminal Live

Before you track the decision, research it.

EdgeDesk prices every NFL and FBS game itself, sets its number beside the sportsbooks’, and tells you how sure it is.

  • EdgeDesk fair lineIts own spread, total and projected score.
  • Market lineWhat the sportsbooks are offering, and when it was captured.
  • Model gapHow far apart EdgeDesk and the market are.
  • Win probabilityFor each side, from EdgeDesk’s own model.
  • ConfidenceA reliability score on college games, and what’s uncertain on every game.
  • Matchup edgeWhere a college team’s units hold the advantage.
  • Roster contextCollege rosters and player ratings; NFL quarterback rooms.
  • WeatherForecasts for college venues, used in the total.
  • Player propsProjections against the line, in an experimental stage.
  • Best available priceWhere supported, the best line on file and the price that still plays.
For advanced users: how the numbers are built

The game model is built from opponent-adjusted efficiency per play, home field and a learned quarterback layer. The prop model is built from volume, share and efficiency, adjusted for matchup, game script, weather and availability. Sportsbook prices are captured with the time they were captured. A language model never produces a probability, a price or a rating.

EdgeDesk EV compares EdgeDesk’s probability with the sportsbook’s break-even at the exact price you’re offered. Expected value estimates the theoretical return of repeatedly taking the same price if EdgeDesk’s probability is accurate — it does not guarantee profit. Raw and calibrated EV are shown separately.

An unconfirmed starting quarterback is carried as unknown, never as healthy. A stale price is not compared. Layers that have not cleared validation move no line.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is EdgeDesk a picks service?

No. Research, not picks. EdgeDesk shows its own price, the market’s price, why they differ and what could make it wrong, and it helps you track and review your own decisions. The choice to bet stays yours — no picks, no locks, no guaranteed winners.

Does EdgeDesk guarantee I’ll make money?

No, and nobody honestly can. EdgeDesk’s numbers are estimates and can be wrong. What it gives you is research before you bet and evidence afterward about how your decisions are actually performing.

Who is EdgeDesk for?

Anyone who wants to approach football betting with more research and a measurable process — from someone who bets $10 on Saturdays to someone who prices every line on the board.

Can beginners use it?

Yes. The core answers are plain English: are you up or down, and are you getting good prices. You never need to know what a metric is called to use it.

Can advanced bettors use it?

Yes. Fair lines, win probabilities, EdgeDesk EV at the exact price, closing-line value with sample sizes and intervals, EdgeDesk’s snapshot at the moment you entered, and every underlying position stay one tap away.

What sports are currently supported?

Football: the NFL and NCAA FBS, the top division of college football — game markets (spreads, moneylines, totals) and player props. Player props are in EdgeDesk’s experimental validation stage, and their EV is labelled model-estimated.

Can I connect my sportsbook or prediction-market account?

Not yet. Today you log your bets yourself. Importing, and connecting sportsbook and prediction-market accounts, are in development, and we will only name a platform as supported once its connection genuinely works.

Which features are still in development?

Process Coach, the Weekly Film Room, the calendar view, per-bet process grades, prediction-market tracking and account connections. Everything on this page marked In development or Soon is not in the product yet; everything else is.

How does the 7-day trial work?

You get everything that’s live for 7 days. Stripe holds a card and charges nothing until day 8. We email you before the trial converts, with the exact date you will be charged, and if you cancel during the trial you are never charged. After the trial, it is $49.99/month until you cancel.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Settings › Subscription inside the app, one click, no conversation. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged; cancel later and access runs to the end of the period you already paid for.
