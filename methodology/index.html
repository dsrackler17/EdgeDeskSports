The game model

A number of its own, for every game.

EdgeDesk builds its own fair spread, fair total, projected score and win probability for every NFL and FBS game from opponent-adjusted efficiency per play, a learned home-field term and a quarterback layer. Player quality, scheme and coaching are shown as research and move no line until they clear validation.

Sportsbook prices are captured with the time they were captured, from every book EdgeDesk reads. The model’s number is set beside the market’s, the gap is sized, and a gap past the integrity guard is investigated as a possible data problem rather than presented as a find. A language model never produces a probability, a price or a rating.

MAX EV is not a recommendation: aggressive lines lean hardest on the tails of the distribution. EdgeDesk EV prices every captured quote at its own line and price — and any line and price you type in.

What EV means. Expected value estimates the theoretical return of repeatedly taking the same price if EdgeDesk’s probability is accurate. It is an estimate — it does not guarantee profit.

It shows its work first. EV is only as good as the probability under it, so the drivers, the uncertainty and the public grading sit beside it, and raw and calibrated EV are shown separately.

The rules are written down. A price qualifies at 1+ point of probability edge, positive EV and no worse than −125 — EdgeDesk’s pre-registered EV policy. It runs in shadow: every read is recorded and graded, and none is presented as a bet.

Why the spread alone isn’t enough. Auburn −8 at −105 and Auburn −8 at −130 are economically different bets. A model edge only becomes a betting edge at a price, so EdgeDesk evaluates the price, not merely the side.

Most prices don’t qualify. On a real slate, PASS is the most common answer. That is a normal result, not a broken one.

Player props

Volume × share × efficiency — then the price.

A player projection is built as team volume (plays per game, opponent pace, the pass rate the game script implies, wind) × the player’s share (carries, targets and red-zone share, recency-weighted and shrunk toward his prior role; teammates ruled out redistribute their share, shown as a projected adjustment) × efficiency (yards per carry, catch rate, yards per reception, completion and touchdown rates, shrunk toward the position by sample size), adjusted for what the opponent allowed by position and for the game environment (implied points from the market, or EdgeDesk’s own line where no market exists). It ends in a distribution, not a number.

That distribution is priced at the exact odds a sportsbook offers: EdgeDesk probability, break-even, fair odds and EV, raw and calibrated kept apart, beside the market’s consensus line and the range of books. Every prop carries its capture time; a price older than 30 minutes is flagged, and past 90 minutes it is treated as stale and no longer priced as current.

Validation stages. Each prop market has a stage — experimental, tracking, research grade, production — set by its own graded record. Today prop markets are experimental: their EV is labelled model-estimated, they inform research and they never carry a stake.

What is not in the feed. Routes run, route participation and offensive-line data are not in EdgeDesk’s data yet, so they are never shown as a reason; snap share is used and labelled as such.

The labels

Four public words. Never a pick.

RESEARCH

Clears EdgeDesk’s research gates: a real model–market disagreement at a current market, with usable data and reliability. Worth opening — not a bet.

WATCH

A real disagreement that has not cleared the gates: an unverified large gap, reliability under the bar, or a price that is ageing.

PASS

EdgeDesk and the market agree, or the price leaves no value. The most common honest answer.

DATA INCOMPLETE

Something EdgeDesk needs is missing or stale — no current market, no projection, a stale price, thin data or an integrity fault — and it says which.

Inside the terminal the decision engine goes one step further and classifies the current price as BET, LEAN, WATCH, PASS or NO DECISION, with the reason and the price that would change the answer. Every threshold is written down and every decision is graded afterwards.

Uncertainty

What EdgeDesk does when it doesn’t know.

  • An unconfirmed starting quarterback is carried as unknown, never as healthy.
  • A stale price is not compared, and a stale prop price is labelled and its EV withheld.
  • A very large gap is investigated as a possible data problem before anything else.
  • Layers that have not cleared validation move no line.
  • Small samples are labelled as small samples.
The public record

Nothing quietly disappears after kickoff.

Every research result is captured before kickoff and frozen with its price and time, graded automatically against the close and the result, and kept — losing stretches included. Open the public record; no account is needed.
