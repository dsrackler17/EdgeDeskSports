How Prediction Radar grades a bettor

  • From their own trades. Profit is computed from every trade and settlement on Polymarket’s public record, never from a third-party ranking.
  • Closing-line value. Did they buy below the last price before the game started?
  • Follow ROI. A follower never gets the bettor’s price, so the record follows each entry at the market price five minutes later and holds it to settlement.
  • Small samples are labelled. Under 50 graded bets, a bettor is listed as a small sample with no rank.
  • A registered test. Whether following the top bettors would have made money was preregistered as H-RADAR-01 and run once on a holdout. What it found is reported as it is, good or bad.
