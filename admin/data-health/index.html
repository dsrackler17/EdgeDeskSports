EdgeDesk data health

Development view

Why college reliability is being lost, and what data work would recover it. Every number here counts what EdgeDesk knows against what it is asked; none of it is a probability, and none of it moves a projection. Reliability diagnostics →

Data bottlenecks — enrichment ROI

For each bottleneck: the games carrying it, the reliability points it costs as scored, what resolving it in full would recover (re-scored with the same simulator the next-action list uses: caps honoured, stability held), a realistic share of that, and the implementation/data cost. Ranked by ROI = estimated recoverable points / cost. An engineering diagnostic, not a betting metric.

Quarterback coverage

Sides on the slate, by the resolver’s confirmation level. Labels, not probabilities.

Availability

Each side’s availability read for its fixture. A provider failure is never read as a healthy roster.

Player ratings

Projected starters (a base 11+11 formation plus K/P) with a MEASURED player-quality rating. An unrated player is not a replacement-level player.

FCS rating bridge

FCS teams rated on EdgeDesk’s own FBS scale through cross-division games. Shadow only: the engine still prices every FCS side from the shared floor.

Markets

Multi-book consensus per game (lib/market_consensus.js). Comparison evidence only.

Reliability with and without the evidence packages

The same games, the same run, the same projections: scored from the enrichment evidence, and from the v2 inputs alone.

Providers

Every source, its health this run, and whose failure it is. A refusal by the run’s own network policy is attributed to the run, not the provider.
