Sending

The launch gate and the kill switches —

Sending is off until it is turned on here. While it is off the pipeline still builds, validates, previews and stores every edition — which is what the pre-launch period should look like. Turning it on is a one-time gate; after that a valid edition sends on its own schedule with no approval.
