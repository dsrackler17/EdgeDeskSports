Want the research behind the number?

The number you are playing against comes out of the same football research terminal EdgeDesk runs for itself: opponent-adjusted team ratings, roster and player continuity, matchup layers, an outcome range, and a model that states plainly what it could not read. It shows its price, the market’s price, the gap between them and the uncertainty around all three.

EdgeDesk is research, not picks. Its projection does not beat the closing line, and a disagreement with the market is a reason to read further — not evidence of an edge.
